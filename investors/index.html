Raising $2,000,000

RizzLingo is the game that teaches you how to talk to people you like.

Cheesy pickup lines and real conversation skills, in four-question levels with XP, streaks, unlockable places and a leaderboard. The mascot is Dave, a camel.

The problem

In Pew Research Center's 2022 survey, 64% of men who had used a dating app in the past year said they felt insecure about how few messages they got. The tools people have either do nothing about that or do the talking for them.

Dating apps

Hand you a match and leave you alone with a blank message box.

AI reply apps

Write the line for you. You learn nothing, and they do not work at a bus stop.

Advice videos

Tell you what to say. Nobody makes you practice it.

The product: practice, disguised as a joke

1 · Learn

Two or three lines per level, from parking tickets to the periodic table.

2 · Get challenged

Four questions. Finish the line, build it, type it, match it. Five hearts.

3 · Unlock places

Earn XP to open the coffee shop, the DMV, the toilet store and the ER.

Half the curriculum is real skill: open questions, reading the room, and taking a no gracefully.

A business can buy the name on a place

Every place in the app has a plain name: the coffee shop, the bagel shop, the toilet store. A business can pay to replace that generic name with its own, on the level, in the lines and on share cards. Sponsored names are labeled as sponsored.

Local

A neighborhood business names the place for players in its city.

National

A popular chain names the place for every player.
